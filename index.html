The simulator controls are intentionally compact while driving. This page shows what each control does.

Press and hold for power; releasing returns the engine to neutral. 10% is approximately an idle docking pulse and 25% is a stronger burst. On twin-engine boats, the centre column operates both engines together. Single-engine boats use one engine column.
Fenders
Deploy either side independently. Fenders cushion side contact and let the hull slide or pivot along a dock with less impact.
Steering
Use the slider for proportional steering, or the buttons for full lock and centre. Sterndrives vector thrust with steering; fixed-shaft inboards use rudders.
Bow Thruster
Available only on equipped boats. Press and hold to push the bow sideways; release to stop.
Docking Lines
Tap a line button near a dock to attach or release it. Bow and stern lines restrain their respective ends; spring lines resist fore-and-aft movement and are useful for pivoting off a dock.
Choose a Level
Arrival levels end when you are in the highlighted berth and secured with at least two lines. Departure levels end when you enter the green exit zone.
Simulator Setup
Choose the boat, marina, starting position, scenario, and how busy the marina should be before you begin. The live simulator screen keeps only the driving controls so it fits better on a phone.
